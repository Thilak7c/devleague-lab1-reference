# Pitch Video Script — 3:00 max

Timed for a 3-minute hard limit. Rehearse against a stopwatch, not against feel.

---

## [0:00–0:25] The Problem (25s)

> Financial teams deal with reports scattered across PDFs, spreadsheets, and
> formats — sometimes scanned, sometimes messy. Reviewing them manually is
> slow, error-prone, and important risks and trends get missed simply
> because no one has time to catch them.

*(Show: a stack/mix of report formats — PDF, CSV, XLSX — cutting to the
upload screen)*

---

## [0:25–0:50] The Solution (25s)

> We built an AI-powered financial report analyzer. Upload any report —
> spreadsheet, text PDF, even a scanned PDF — and it extracts the data,
> detects anomalies, and shows you exactly what changed and why, on one
> dashboard.

*(Show: drag-and-drop upload, processing state)*

---

## [0:50–1:50] Live Demo (60s) — the core of the pitch

> Here's a real quarterly report. [Upload] It extracts every line item
> automatically. [Point to dashboard] Three insights flagged — a 340%
> marketing spend spike, a statistical outlier in one vendor's invoice, and
> a duplicate entry. Every single one links back to the exact source rows —
> nothing here is a black box.
>
> It also handles personal information automatically — [point to masking
> badge] two fields were masked before this data ever reached analysis or
> the screen. And if a report is a scanned image with no text layer at all
> [upload scanned sample] — same result, same explainability, powered by
> AI-assisted image reading.

*(Show: full flow — upload → insights → drill-down → masking badge →
scanned-PDF path if time allows)*

---

## [1:50–2:20] What Makes This Different (30s)

> Two things set this apart. First, explainability — every insight traces
> to real rows, not an LLM summary you have to trust blindly. Second, PDPA
> compliance is a first-class feature, not an afterthought — nothing is
> stored beyond the session, and personal data is actively detected and
> masked, visibly, in the UI.

*(Show: insight drill-down + privacy notice)*

---

## [2:20–2:45] Impact (25s)

> This turns a manual review process that takes hours into something that
> takes seconds — while catching things a tired reviewer might miss, and
> doing it in a way that's transparent and compliant by design.

---

## [2:45–3:00] Close (15s)

> [Product name] — faster financial insight, without sacrificing trust or
> compliance. Thank you.

*(Show: closing frame — logo/team names)*
